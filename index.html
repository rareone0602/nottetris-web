fill a row

81% will do, at any angle.

Move with the left and right arrows. Hold Z or X to turn. Hold down or space to fall faster. P or Escape pauses, and Enter starts. On a touch screen, the controls are under the board. Pieces keep moving after they land.

how to play

Move the falling piece, and hold a turn key to rotate it freely. It keeps sliding and turning for a moment after you let go, so short taps steer best. The next piece arrives when yours touches the pile. Landed pieces can still tip, slide and get in the way.

Fill more than 81% of a row. The marks to the right of the board show how full each row is; a tick means the row goes at the next landing. A full row is crossed out and cut away, even through a tilted piece, and whatever was above falls into the gap. Ten rows raise the level, and the pieces fall a little faster.

The dashed line near the top is the limit. A piece that lands with its middle above it ends the game, and the line darkens when the pile gets close.

where it came from

Not Tetris 2 was made by Stabyourself. Its freely rotating blocks and area-based line cutting are reproduced here in JavaScript. This is an independent browser adaptation, with a paper-and-pencil page, touch controls and a slower setting.
