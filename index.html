hold a word to move or turn. two thumbs work.

Move with the left and right arrows. Hold Z or X to turn. Hold down or space to fall faster. P or Escape pauses. Touch controls are below the board. Pieces keep moving after they land.

how to play

Move the falling piece and hold a turn key to rotate it freely. The next piece arrives when yours touches the pile. Landed pieces can still tip, slide and get in the way.

Fill more than 81% of a row. The little marks beside the board show how close each row is. A full row is sliced out, even through a tilted piece; the rest falls into the gap. Ten cleared rows raise the level.

where it came from

Not Tetris 2 was made by Stabyourself. Its freely rotating blocks and area-based line cutting are reproduced here in JavaScript. This is an independent browser adaptation, with a paper-and-pencil page, touch controls and a slower setting.
